The Game Loop

A 5–15 minute run. The core tension is the fork at the end of every fight: push one more, or cash out? Death forfeits the run's haul, so every extra hunt is a bet.

01
Choose zone
Start Lv.1 with kept gear. Deeper = bigger gaps, bigger risk.
▸
02
Read the map
A few fights, each with visible level, reward & risk.
▸
03
Win the HP race
Dodge, break parts, maybe spend a Trap to capture.
▸
04
Explode in XP
A big gap dumps thousands of levels at once.
✗ OVER-REACH → DIE
Lose the race and you forfeit everything gathered this run — materials, essence, captures. Only already-crafted gear survives.
✓ RETREAT → BANK
Cash out (or finish your hunts) to bank the haul + essence scaled to your peak level. The safe, deliberate call.
↺  Reset to Lv.1 · spend essence on modest upgrades · craft new gear · launch farther than last time
03

What Keeps It Honest

SPINE

The wall holds

Chip damage scales with a monster's power, so no amount of dodging beats the Lv-9,999,999,999 Voidmaw until your stats catch up.

CARRY

Gear over grind

Crafted gear is the real between-run power. Permanent upgrades only smooth the early climb, never trivialize the build.

DEPTH

Every layer is a choice

Weapon feel, elemental matchups, armor skills, and your one companion each bend a run without becoming a second game.
